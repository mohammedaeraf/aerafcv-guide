
# **Aeraf CV – Instagram Posting Plan**

Social Media Marketing Team Guidelines | Monthly Content Strategy

## **1. Objectives**

The primary objectives of Aeraf CV's Instagram account are to:

1. Build brand awareness and establish credibility.
2. Educate job seekers about professional resume writing.
3. Showcase the quality of Aeraf CV's resume services.
4. Generate enquiries from freshers and experienced professionals.
5. Convert Instagram followers into paying customers.

**Target audience:**

- Fresh graduates and job seekers.
- Experienced professionals looking for career growth.
- People planning to change jobs or careers.
- Professionals in India and the UAE.

## **2. Posting frequency**

We should maintain a consistent posting schedule rather than posting too frequently.

| Content type      | Frequency           |
| ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Main feed posts   | 3 per week          |
| Instagram Stories | 3–4 days per week   |
| Reels             | At least 1 per week |
| Carousel posts    | 1–2 per week        |
| Promotional posts | 1 per week          |

**Recommended schedule**

**Monday**

Educational

Resume tips, common mistakes, ATS guidance and career advice.

**Wednesday**

Showcase

Sample resumes, before-and-after examples, resume sections and design ideas.

**Friday**

Marketing

Aeraf CV services, packages, customer benefits, testimonials and enquiry prompts.

Suggested posting time: Test 12 PM–2 PM and 6 PM–9 PM IST initially. Review Instagram Insights after a few weeks and adjust based on audience activity.

## **5. Content format guidelines**

| Format       | Recommended approach                        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Carousel     | 4–6 slides explaining one topic             |
| Reels        | 20–40 seconds with clear text and narration |
| Single image | Announcements, offers and short tips        |
| Stories      | Polls, quizzes, questions and reminders     |

**Carousel example – 5 Common Resume Mistakes**

1. Slide 1: 5 Common Resume Mistakes.
2. Slide 2: Using the same resume for every job.
3. Slide 3: Writing lengthy, unclear descriptions.
4. Slide 4: Including irrelevant information.
5. Slide 5: Ignoring ATS-friendly formatting.
6. Final CTA: Need professional resume assistance? Contact Aeraf CV.

## **8. Instagram Stories strategy**

Stories should be used to maintain regular interaction between main feed posts.

Examples:

- Monday: Resume tip of the day.
- Tuesday: Poll – "Do you have an ATS-friendly resume?"
- Thursday: Quick career tip or quiz.
- Saturday: Service reminder or customer feedback.

Use interactive features such as polls, question stickers and quizzes.

## **10. Monthly performance tracking**

The marketing team should submit a short report at the end of every month.

| Metric         | What to track                     |
| -------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Followers      | New followers gained              |
| Reach          | Unique accounts reached           |
| Engagement     | Likes, comments, shares and saves |
| Reels          | Views and average watch time      |
| Profile visits | Number of profile visits          |
| Website clicks | Visitors from Instagram           |
| Enquiries      | Genuine customer enquiries        |
| Conversions    | Paid customers generated          |

Suggested initial working targets:

- 12 main feed posts per month.
- 12–16 Stories per month.
- At least 4 Reels per month.
- 5–10 genuine enquiries per month.

These are starting targets, not guaranteed results. Review and adjust them after the first month.
