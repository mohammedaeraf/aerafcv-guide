Today we learn how to identify, organise and present candidate skills professionally.

We will cover:

✅ Technical skills vs soft skills\
✅ Job-specific and transferable skills\
✅ Identifying skills from education and work experience\
✅ Organising skills into categories\
✅ Tailoring skills to the target job\
✅ Common mistakes and unsupported skill claims
