📝 _Today's Assignment:_
Choose one of our fictional candidates (Fresher, Office Administrator, Sales Executive or Career Changer) and prepare a resume draft incorporating all relevant sections.

🔍 Review your resume for:
• Accuracy and honesty
• Relevance to the target job
• Consistent formatting
• Clear section headings
• Missing information that needs confirmation

🎯 _Bonus Challenge:_ Exchange your resume draft with another trainee and identify at least 3 improvements.

📌 _Remember:_ A good resume isn't just about filling sections. It is about telling a clear, credible and compelling professional story.
