## Yousuf has interest in Sales

### Can we position Yousuf for an entry-level sales opportunity at **Etisalat (e&) UAE** 

#### Yes, we can position Yousuf for an entry-level sales opportunity at **Etisalat (e&) UAE** without pretending that he has prior sales experience.

The key is to present him as a **customer-oriented, tech-savvy graduate with research, communication, presentation and persuasion-related skills who is willing to build a career in sales.**

His BCA background and content strategy experience can be useful differentiators.

Recommended positioning

Motivated BCA graduate with experience in content strategy, audience research, competitor analysis and creative pitching. Interested in building a career in telecommunications sales, customer engagement and business development.

- Sales Executive
