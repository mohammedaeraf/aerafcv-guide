# Aeraf CV – Resume Writer Training
## Day 1: Resume Writing – The Fundamentals

**Module:** 1 – Resume & CV Fundamentals  
**Learning Time:** 10–15 minutes

### Learning Objective

Understand what a resume is, why it is important, what makes a good resume and the different types of resumes.

---

### 1. What is a Resume?

A resume is a professional document that presents a candidate's:

- Educational qualifications
- Work experience
- Skills and abilities
- Achievements
- Professional background

Its purpose is to communicate why a candidate may be suitable for a particular job.

**Remember:** A resume is not simply a list of everything a person has done. It is a carefully organised presentation of the most relevant qualifications and experience for a target role.

### 2. Resume vs CV

The terms Resume and CV are often used interchangeably in India and many other countries.

Traditionally:

- **Resume:** A concise document, usually 1–2 pages, tailored to a particular job.
- **CV (Curriculum Vitae):** A more detailed record of academic and professional history, often used in academic and research careers.

For Aeraf CV, we will primarily focus on professional resumes for freshers and experienced job seekers.

### 3. Types of Resumes

**A. Chronological Resume**

Lists work experience in reverse chronological order (most recent job first).

Suitable for candidates with consistent work experience.

**B. Functional Resume**

Focuses on skills and abilities rather than employment history.

Suitable in certain cases involving career gaps or career changes, although some recruiters prefer a clear employment history.

**C. Combination Resume**

Combines a skills-focused section with a chronological employment history.

Suitable for experienced candidates who want to highlight both their skills and work experience.

### 4. Characteristics of a Good Resume

A good resume should be:

1. **Relevant:** Tailored to the target job.
2. **Clear:** Easy to read and understand.
3. **Concise:** Avoids unnecessary information.
4. **Accurate:** Contains genuine and verifiable information.
5. **Professional:** Consistent formatting and language.
6. **ATS-friendly:** Uses a readable structure that applicant tracking systems can process.

### 5. Example: A Weak vs Strong Resume Statement

**Weak statement:**

"I am hardworking and looking for a good job."

**Stronger statement:**

"Detail-oriented administrative professional with experience in record management, data entry and day-to-day office coordination."

The second statement is more professional because it communicates specific skills and experience.

### 6. Practical Exercise

Consider this fictional candidate:

**Candidate: Rahul Sharma**

- Education: B.Com graduate
- Experience: Fresher
- Skills: MS Excel, MS Word, Data Entry
- Target role: Office Assistant

Answer these questions:

1. Which resume type would you recommend for Rahul?
2. What are the three most relevant skills to highlight?
3. Should Rahul include work experience if he has never worked professionally?
4. Write a two-sentence professional summary for Rahul.

### 7. Today's Assignment

Find any two publicly available sample resumes online:

- One for a fresher.
- One for an experienced professional.

Examine their structure and identify the main differences.

**Note:** Do not copy their content. Focus on understanding how information is organised.

### 8. Key Takeaways

- A resume is a professional marketing document.
- It should be tailored to the target job.
- Different candidates may require different resume structures.
- Accuracy and readability are more important than decorative design.
- Never invent qualifications, skills or achievements.

---

**Aeraf CV Training Principle:**

Our responsibility is not just to make a resume look attractive. It is to present the candidate's genuine professional strengths effectively.
