## **4. Important Resume Quality Checks**

Before finalising any resume, check the following:

1. **Accuracy:** Are all qualifications, dates, skills and experiences verified?
2. **Consistency:** Are date formats, headings, fonts and spacing consistent?
3. **Relevance:** Does the content match the candidate's target role?
4. **Completeness:** Are any important details missing?
5. **Readability:** Can a recruiter quickly understand the candidate's profile?
6. **Honesty:** Have we avoided exaggerating skills or inventing achievements?

Never create fake experience, certifications, projects, metrics or qualifications to make a resume look impressive.

## **5. Short Knowledge Check**

Try answering these questions without referring to previous lessons.

1. What is the difference between a professional summary and a career objective?
2. Should a fresher include academic projects in their resume?
3. Should we list every skill a candidate claims to know, regardless of proficiency?
4. Where should professional certifications and short workshops be listed?
5. What should we do if a candidate has not provided their previous employer's joining or leaving dates?

**Suggested answers:**

1. A summary highlights the candidate's background and strengths; an objective communicates career direction.
2. Yes, if relevant to the target role.
3. No. Skills should be genuine and defensible.
4. Under Certifications, Courses or Additional Training, with their nature clearly identified.
5. Ask the candidate to confirm the dates rather than guessing.

## **7. Assignment**

Submit a draft resume containing all relevant sections for your selected candidate.

**Submission requirements:**

- Use clear section headings.
- Maintain consistent formatting.
- Tailor the summary and skills to the target role.
- Do not invent missing information.
- Highlight any information that needs confirmation from the candidate.

**Bonus challenge:** Exchange your draft with another trainee. Ask them to review it and identify at least three improvements.

## **8. Key Takeaways**

- A good resume tells a coherent professional story.
- Every section should contribute useful information.
- Resume structure should reflect the candidate's experience and career stage.
- Relevant information is more valuable than unnecessary content.
- Accuracy and honesty are more important than making a resume look impressive.
- Always review the complete resume before delivering it to a client.
