## 1. Learning Objective

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Understand the purpose of a resume headline.
- Write a professional and relevant resume headline.
- Differentiate between a professional summary and a career objective.
- Write effective summaries for freshers and experienced professionals.
- Avoid generic, exaggerated or misleading statements.

---

## 2. What Is a Resume Headline?

A resume headline is a short statement placed near the top of a resume, usually below the candidate's name and contact information.

It communicates the candidate's professional identity or target role.

It helps recruiters quickly understand what type of professional they are looking at.

### Examples

**For a Fresher:**

BCA Graduate | Aspiring Junior Web Developer

**For an Office Administrator:**

Office Administrator | Records Management | Office Coordination

**For a Sales Professional:**

Sales Associate | Customer Service | Retail Operations

**For a Career Changer:**

Customer Support Professional Transitioning to HR | Coordination | Communication

### Guidelines for Writing Headlines

1. Keep the headline short and clear.
2. Include the relevant job title or professional identity.
3. Include one or two relevant specialisations or skills if useful.
4. Avoid unnecessary adjectives such as "Dynamic", "Superstar" or "World-Class".
5. Never use an inflated designation that the candidate has not held.

**Remember:** A headline should immediately communicate professional relevance, not advertise unrealistic claims.

---

## 3. What Is a Professional Summary?

A professional summary is a short paragraph that introduces the candidate's qualifications, experience, relevant skills and professional strengths.

It usually contains 2–4 concise lines.

It answers three important questions:

- Who is the candidate professionally?
- What relevant experience or capabilities do they have?
- What value can they bring to the target role?

### Example – Experienced Office Administrator

"Office Administrator with experience supporting administrative operations, records management, correspondence and meeting coordination. Proficient in routine MS Office tasks and document handling, with a focus on accurate records and dependable office support."

This summary communicates:
- Professional identity.
- Relevant experience.
- Key skills.
- Professional strengths.

Notice that it does not contain unnecessary personal statements.

### Example – Sales Professional

"Retail sales professional with experience assisting customers, presenting product options, processing orders and supporting store operations. Skilled in customer communication, product presentation, POS billing and stock coordination."

This is more useful than simply writing:

"I am a hardworking and dedicated person looking for a good job in a reputed company."

---

## 4. What Is a Career Objective?

A career objective is a short statement describing the type of professional opportunity the candidate is seeking.

It is generally more useful for:
- Fresh graduates.
- Candidates entering the job market.
- Candidates transitioning into a new career.
- Candidates with limited relevant work experience.

### Example – Fresher

"Seeking a Junior Web Developer opportunity to apply foundational knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React while contributing to the development of responsive web applications and gaining practical industry experience."

### Example – Career Changer

"Seeking an entry-level opportunity in Human Resources or Recruitment where communication, coordination and documentation skills can be applied while developing professional HR expertise."

**Important:** A career objective should focus on the target opportunity and relevant capabilities. Avoid vague statements such as "seeking a challenging position in a reputed organisation."

---

## 5. Professional Summary vs Career Objective

These two sections are often confused by beginner resume writers.

| Professional Summary | Career Objective |
|---|---|
| Focuses on existing experience and strengths | Focuses on the desired opportunity |
| Highlights professional value | Highlights career direction |
| Commonly suitable for experienced professionals | Often useful for freshers and career changers |
| Describes what the candidate brings | Describes what the candidate is seeking |

### Which One Should We Use?

**Freshers:** A short career objective or a skills-focused professional summary can be used.

**Experienced Professionals:** A professional summary is generally preferable.

**Career Changers:** A professional summary highlighting transferable skills is often effective. An objective can also be used if it adds useful clarity.

There is no requirement to include both sections in every resume.

## 6. Practical Examples Using Our Four Candidate Profiles

Let's apply today's concepts to our fictional candidates.

### Candidate A – Aisha Rahman (Fresher)

**Target Role:** Junior Web Developer

**Suggested Headline:**

BCA Graduate | Aspiring Junior Frontend Developer

**Suggested Professional Summary:**

BCA graduate with foundational knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React. Developed a responsive campus events interface featuring event listings, category filtering and detail views. Seeking a junior frontend development opportunity to build practical software development experience.

**Alternative Career Objective:**

Seeking a Junior Frontend Developer position to apply foundational web development knowledge and project experience while contributing to responsive and user-friendly web applications.

**Writer's Note:** Highlight education, technical skills and academic projects instead of employment experience.

### Candidate B – Rahul Shetty (Office Administrator)

**Target Role:** Office Administrator

**Suggested Headline:**

Office Administrator | Administrative Operations | Records Management

**Suggested Professional Summary:**

Office Administrator with experience supporting administrative operations, records management, correspondence, meeting coordination and spreadsheet updates. Proficient in routine MS Office tasks and document handling, with a focus on accurate records and dependable office support.

**Writer's Note:** A professional summary is more suitable than a generic career objective because Rahul already has relevant work experience.

### Candidate C – Naveen Kumar (Sales Professional)

**Target Role:** Sales Executive

**Suggested Headline:**

Retail Sales Professional | Customer Service | Product Presentation

**Suggested Professional Summary:**

Retail sales professional with experience assisting customers, presenting product options, processing orders and supporting store operations. Skilled in customer communication, product presentation, POS billing and stock coordination. Seeking a Sales Executive opportunity in retail or consumer products.

**Writer's Note:** Emphasise customer interaction and sales-related responsibilities. Do not invent sales targets or achievements.

### Candidate D – Priya Nair (Career Changer)

**Target Role:** HR / Recruitment Coordinator

**Suggested Headline:**

Customer Support Professional Transitioning to HR | Communication | Coordination

**Suggested Professional Summary:**

Customer support professional with experience handling customer enquiries, maintaining CRM records, coordinating escalations and supporting customer onboarding. Brings transferable skills in communication, scheduling, record accuracy and stakeholder coordination, supported by additional training in HR Fundamentals. Seeking an entry-level opportunity in HR or recruitment coordination.

**Writer's Note:** Show how her previous experience relates to HR responsibilities. Do not present her as an experienced HR professional or recruiter.

## 7. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Incorrect Approach | Better Approach |
|---|---|
| Hardworking and dedicated person | Identify relevant professional skills |
| Seeking a challenging position | Mention the specific target role |
| Expert in all technologies | Mention only verified technical skills |
| Highly experienced professional (for a fresher) | BCA Graduate / Aspiring Developer |
| Excellent sales performer without evidence | Describe actual sales responsibilities |
| Experienced HR professional (for Priya) | Customer Support Professional Transitioning to HR |

### Ethical Writing Rule

Never exaggerate the candidate's experience, skills or professional achievements to make a resume look impressive.

A professional resume must remain truthful and verifiable.

---

## 10. Key Takeaways

- A resume headline communicates the candidate's professional identity or target role.
- A professional summary highlights existing experience, skills and professional value.
- A career objective explains the type of opportunity the candidate is seeking.
- Experienced professionals generally benefit from professional summaries.
- Freshers may use a career objective or a skills-focused summary.
- Career changers should highlight transferable skills.
- Avoid generic statements, exaggerated claims and unnecessary adjectives.
- Always tailor the headline and summary to the target job.

---

### AERAF CV – PROFESSIONAL WRITING PRINCIPLE

**"Your first few lines should make the recruiter understand who you are, what you offer and why you are relevant to the position."**

A good headline attracts attention. A good summary builds interest. Both must be supported by genuine candidate information.
