# **Aeraf CV – Resume Writer Training**

DAY 3 · MODULE 1

## **Lesson 3: Choosing the Right Resume Format for Different Candidates**

10–15 minutes

Beginner level

### **Learning Objective**

Understand the three major resume formats and learn how to select the most suitable format based on a candidate's education, experience, skills and career goals.

### **1. What is a Resume Format?**

A resume format refers to the way a candidate's professional information is organised and presented.

Choosing the right format is important because different candidates have different backgrounds.

For example:

- A fresher may want to highlight education and projects.
- An experienced professional may want to highlight work experience.
- A career changer may want to highlight transferable skills.

A resume writer should not use the same format for every candidate.

### **2. The Three Major Resume Formats**

#### **A. Reverse-Chronological Resume**

This is the most commonly recommended format for professional resumes.

It presents the candidate's employment history starting with the most recent job and moving backwards.

**Example:**

**Professional Experience**

Senior Accountant – ABC Technologies (2023–Present)

Accountant – XYZ Solutions (2020–2023)

Junior Accountant – PQR Enterprises (2018–2020)

**Advantages:**

- Easy for recruiters to understand.
- Clearly shows career progression.
- Highlights recent professional experience.
- Generally compatible with ATS systems when formatted properly.

**Suitable for:**

- Experienced professionals.
- Candidates with a consistent employment history.
- Candidates applying for roles related to their previous experience.

#### **B. Functional Resume**

This format focuses primarily on skills and abilities rather than presenting employment history as the main feature.

**Example:**

**Key Areas of Expertise**

- Customer Relationship Management
- Sales and Negotiation
- Team Coordination
- Business Development

**Professional Background**

Previous employment details are listed separately, usually with less emphasis.

**Advantages:**

- Highlights skills and competencies.
- Can help candidates explain transferable abilities.

**Limitations:**

- Recruiters may find it difficult to understand the candidate's employment history.
- Some recruiters and ATS systems may not process heavily rearranged information as effectively.
- It can create questions about unexplained employment gaps.

**Suitable for:**

Certain career changers or candidates with diverse experience, although a combination format is often a better choice.

#### **C. Combination Resume**

This format combines the strengths of reverse-chronological and functional resumes.

It highlights important skills first, followed by detailed employment history in reverse chronological order.

**Example:**

**Professional Summary**

Experienced sales professional with 5 years of experience in customer relationship management and business development.

**Core Competencies**

- Sales Management
- Customer Relationship Management
- Negotiation
- Lead Generation

**Professional Experience**

Sales Executive – ABC Ltd. (2023–Present)

Business Development Executive – XYZ Ltd. (2021–2023)

**Advantages:**

- Highlights important skills.
- Maintains a clear employment history.
- Helps demonstrate transferable skills.
- Suitable for candidates with diverse professional experience.

**Suitable for:**

- Career changers.
- Experienced professionals with diverse skills.
- Candidates applying for roles requiring a different combination of competencies.

### **3. Which Resume Format Should We Choose?**

Let's consider four fictional candidate profiles used in our Aeraf CV training.

| Candidate    | Background                                     | Recommended format                                                      |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rahul Sharma | B.Com fresher                                  | Reverse-chronological structure with education and projects highlighted |
| Priya Nair   | Office Administrator, 5 years' experience      | Reverse-chronological                                                   |
| Arjun Kumar  | Sales Executive, 7 years' experience           | Reverse-chronological                                                   |
| Sameer Khan  | IT professional moving into project management | Combination                                                             |

**Important:** A career changer does not necessarily need a functional resume. A combination resume usually allows us to highlight transferable skills while maintaining a transparent employment history.

### **4. Resume Format vs Resume Design**

These two terms should not be confused.

**Resume Format:** How information is organised.

Examples:

- Reverse-chronological
- Functional
- Combination

**Resume Design:** How the resume looks visually.

Examples:

- Simple professional layout
- Modern two-column layout
- Canva-designed resume
- Minimalist resume

A resume can have a reverse-chronological format with either a simple or modern visual design.

For Aeraf CV, we should prioritise readability, relevance and ATS compatibility over decorative design.

### **5. Practical Exercise**

Consider the following candidates.

**Candidate A – Fresh Graduate**

- BCA graduate
- No professional work experience
- Knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React
- Completed two academic projects

**Candidate B – Experienced Accountant**

- 8 years of accounting experience
- Worked in three companies
- Currently working as a Senior Accountant

**Candidate C – Career Changer**

- 6 years of experience in customer support
- Wants to move into Human Resource Management
- Has experience in communication, conflict resolution and team coordination

Answer the following:

1. Which resume format would you recommend for Candidate A?
2. Why is reverse-chronological suitable for Candidate B?
3. Which format would you recommend for Candidate C?
4. Should Candidate C remove previous employment details from the resume?
5. What is more important when selecting a resume format: the candidate's preference or the requirements of the target job?

### **6. Today's Assignment**

Prepare a small comparison table for the four fictional Aeraf CV candidates:

- Rahul Sharma – Fresher
- Priya Nair – Office Administrator
- Arjun Kumar – Sales Executive
- Sameer Khan – Career Changer

For each candidate, mention:

1. Recommended resume format.
2. Three important sections to highlight.
3. Reason for selecting that format.

Do not create complete resumes yet. Focus only on selecting the appropriate structure.

### **7. Key Takeaways**

- There are three major resume formats: reverse-chronological, functional and combination.
- Reverse-chronological is generally the preferred format for candidates with relevant employment history.
- Functional resumes focus on skills but may make employment history less clear.
- Combination resumes are useful for candidates with transferable skills and diverse experience.
- Freshers should highlight education, projects, internships and relevant skills.
- Always select the resume format based on the candidate's background and target job.

**Aeraf CV Quality Principle:**

*Every candidate has a different professional story. Our job is to choose a resume structure that communicates that story clearly, honestly and effectively.*
