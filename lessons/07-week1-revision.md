### 2. Five Important Principles

**1. Understand before writing**

Never start writing a resume without understanding the candidate's background and target job.

**2. Choose the right format**

- Fresher: Usually reverse-chronological or skills-focused.
- Experienced professional: Usually reverse-chronological.
- Career changer: A combination format can help highlight transferable skills.

**3. Focus on relevance**

A resume should highlight the candidate's most relevant qualifications and experience, not simply list everything they have ever done.

**4. Keep it clear and professional**

Use simple language, meaningful headings, consistent formatting and concise bullet points.

**5. Maintain accuracy and honesty**

Never invent qualifications, employment history, skills, achievements or performance figures. Ask the candidate for clarification when information is missing.

### 3. Practical Exercise – Analyse Four Candidates

Use the four fictional candidate profiles from our workbook.

| Candidate | Target Role | Main Resume Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Aisha Rahman | Junior Web Developer | BCA education, technical skills and React project |
| Rahul Shetty | Office Administrator | Administrative experience, documentation and coordination |
| Naveen Kumar | Sales Executive | Customer service, product presentation and sales support |
| Priya Nair | HR/Recruitment Coordinator | Transferable skills, customer support and HR course |

**Your task:** For each candidate, identify:

1. Which resume format would you recommend?
2. What are the three most relevant strengths to highlight?
3. What should the resume headline communicate?
4. What additional information would you ask the candidate to provide?

*Example – Aisha Rahman*

- **Format:** Reverse-chronological with a strong skills and projects section.
- **Strengths:** BCA qualification, HTML/CSS/JavaScript knowledge, React-based Campus Events Portal.
- **Headline:** BCA Graduate | Junior Frontend Developer | HTML, CSS, JavaScript & React.
- **Additional information:** Project details, GitHub portfolio link, internship experience (if any), and specific contributions to the project.

### 5. Small Assignment – Create a Resume Strategy

Choose **any ONE** of the four fictional candidates from the workbook.

Prepare a short resume strategy containing:

1. Candidate name and target job.
2. Recommended resume format.
3. Three key skills or strengths to highlight.
4. A suitable resume headline.
5. A professional summary of 3–4 sentences.
6. Three questions you would ask the candidate before finalising the resume.

Keep your answers concise. You do not need to create the complete resume yet.

### 6. Ethical Reminder

Throughout this programme, remember the Aeraf CV standards:

- Be truthful and accurate.
- Never fabricate qualifications, experience or achievements.
- Verify candidate information before including it.
- Protect candidate contact details and confidential information.
- Avoid unnecessary personal information.
- Never disclose one client's resume or personal details to another client.

**A good resume writer improves how a candidate's real experience is presented. A good resume writer never changes the truth.**

### Key Takeaways

- Understand the candidate and target job before writing.
- Choose a resume format that suits the candidate.
- Highlight relevant skills, qualifications and achievements.
- Write clear headlines and professional summaries.
- Review every resume for relevance, readability and accuracy.
