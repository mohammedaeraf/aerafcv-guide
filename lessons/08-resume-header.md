# Aeraf CV – Resume Writer Training Programme
## Day 8 – Writing the Contact Information & Resume Header
**Module:** Week 2 – Resume Sections  
**Duration:** 10–15 minutes

### 🎯 Learning Objective

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Create a professional resume header.
- Identify the essential contact details to include.
- Present LinkedIn, GitHub and portfolio links appropriately.
- Avoid unnecessary personal information.
- Check contact information for accuracy and readability.

---

### 1. What Is a Resume Header?

The resume header is the first section a recruiter sees. It usually contains the candidate's name, professional headline and contact information.

Its purpose is simple: help the recruiter identify the candidate and contact them easily.

A good header should be:

- Clear and professional.
- Easy to read.
- Concise.
- Consistently formatted.
- Suitable for the candidate's target role.

### 2. Essential Information to Include

| Information | Recommendation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Full name | Include prominently | Aisha Rahman |
| Phone number | Include a working number with country code where relevant | +91 90000 11111 |
| Professional email | Use a simple, professional email address | aisha.rahman@example.com |
| Location | City and state/country are generally sufficient | Mangaluru, Karnataka |
| LinkedIn | Include if the profile is complete and relevant | linkedin.com/in/username |
| Portfolio/GitHub | Include when relevant to the target role | github.com/username |

**Important:** Do not invent missing contact details or links. Ask the candidate to provide and verify them.

### 3. Writing a Professional Headline

A headline is a short description of the candidate's professional identity or target role.

It is different from the candidate's name and should not be confused with the professional summary.

Examples:

**For a fresher:**

BCA Graduate | Junior Frontend Developer | HTML, CSS, JavaScript & React

**For an experienced administrator:**

Office Administrator | Documentation, Scheduling & Office Coordination

**For a sales professional:**

Sales Professional | Customer Service | Product Presentation & Retail Operations

**For a career changer:**

Customer Support Professional Transitioning to HR | Communication & Coordination

Notice that each headline highlights relevant strengths without making unsupported claims.

### 4. What Should We Avoid?

A resume header should not contain unnecessary personal information.

Generally, avoid including:

- Religion or caste.
- Marital status.
- Passport or national identification numbers.
- Full residential address.
- Date of birth, unless specifically required for a legitimate reason.
- Personal social media accounts unrelated to the job.
- Unprofessional email addresses.

A photograph is not universally required. Follow the employer's instructions and the relevant country's hiring conventions rather than adding one automatically.

**Ethical rule:** Protect the candidate's privacy. Collect and include only information that is genuinely needed.

### 5. Practical Example – Aisha Rahman

Aisha is a BCA graduate applying for Junior Web Developer positions.

A possible resume header using the information available in our workbook:

**AISHA RAHMAN**

BCA Graduate | Junior Frontend Developer | HTML, CSS, JavaScript & React

Mangaluru, Karnataka  
+91 90000 11111 | aisha.rahman@example.com

GitHub: To be provided, if available  
LinkedIn: To be provided, if available

The last two lines are reminders for the resume writer, not content to leave in the final resume. Ask Aisha for her actual profile links and include them only after checking that they work and are relevant.

### 6. Formatting Tips

A few simple formatting rules make a header look professional:

1. Use the candidate's name as the most prominent text.
2. Keep the headline directly below the name.
3. Place contact details on one or two readable lines.
4. Use consistent separators such as `|` between contact details.
5. Use clickable hyperlinks where appropriate, but make sure the visible link is understandable.
6. Avoid icons or decorative graphics that could interfere with ATS readability.
7. Check that phone numbers, email addresses and links are correct.

**Remember:** A beautiful header is not useful if the recruiter cannot find or use the contact details.

### 7. Short Exercise – Identify the Problems

Review this sample header:

**RAHUL**

Looking for any job  
rahulcoolboy1990@example.com  
Abu Dhabi  
Married | Indian | Date of Birth: 15/05/1990  
Phone: 0000000000

Identify at least **five problems** with this header.

*Hint:* Consider the name, headline, email, contact details and unnecessary personal information.

### 8. Small Assignment – Prepare Four Resume Headers

Using the four fictional profiles from our workbook, prepare a professional resume header for each candidate:

1. Aisha Rahman – Fresher / Junior Web Developer.
2. Rahul Shetty – Office Administrator.
3. Naveen Kumar – Sales Executive.
4. Priya Nair – Career Changer / HR Coordinator.

For each candidate, include:

- Full name.
- Suitable professional headline.
- Location.
- Phone number.
- Email address.
- Relevant professional links, if available.

If a detail is missing, write down what you would ask the candidate. Do not make up information.

### Key Takeaways

- The resume header creates the recruiter's first impression.
- Include accurate contact details and a relevant professional headline.
- Use professional email addresses.
- Add LinkedIn, GitHub or portfolio links only when relevant and verified.
- Avoid unnecessary personal and sensitive information.
- Always verify contact details before delivering the resume.
