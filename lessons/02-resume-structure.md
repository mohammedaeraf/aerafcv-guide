# Aeraf CV – Resume Writer Training
## Day 2: Understanding the Structure of a Professional Resume

**Module:** 1 – Resume & CV Fundamentals  
**Learning Time:** 10–15 minutes

### Learning Objective

Understand the different sections of a professional resume, their purpose and the recommended order in which they should appear.

---

### 1. The Main Sections of a Professional Resume

A typical professional resume contains the following sections:

#### 1. Contact Information

Includes:

- Full name
- Phone number
- Professional email address
- City and country
- LinkedIn profile URL (if available)

Avoid including unnecessary personal information such as religion, marital status or passport details unless specifically required for a legitimate purpose.

#### 2. Professional Summary

A short introduction highlighting the candidate's experience, key skills and professional strengths.

**Example:**

"Motivated B.Com graduate with knowledge of MS Excel, data entry and administrative operations. Possesses good organisational and communication skills, seeking an opportunity to contribute to an office administration team."

#### 3. Key Skills

A list of relevant skills that match the target job.

**Example for an Office Assistant:**

- MS Excel
- Data Entry
- Record Management
- Email Communication
- Document Preparation
- Office Administration

#### 4. Work Experience

Includes:

- Job title
- Company name
- Location
- Employment period
- Key responsibilities and achievements

**Example:**

**Office Assistant | ABC Solutions, Mangalore**  
January 2023 – Present

- Maintained office records and organised documentation.
- Assisted with data entry and preparation of reports.
- Coordinated daily administrative activities.

#### 5. Education

Includes:

- Qualification
- Institution name
- Year of completion
- Relevant grades, if useful

**Example:**

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com)  
Mangalore University | 2024

#### 6. Certifications and Training

Include relevant professional certifications, workshops and training programmes.

#### 7. Additional Information

Depending on the candidate and target role, this may include:

- Languages
- Technical tools
- Projects
- Volunteer experience
- Relevant achievements

---

### 2. Recommended Order of Resume Sections

**For an Experienced Professional:**

1. Name and Contact Information
2. Professional Summary
3. Key Skills
4. Work Experience
5. Education
6. Certifications
7. Additional Information

**For a Fresher:**

1. Name and Contact Information
2. Professional Summary or Career Objective
3. Education
4. Key Skills
5. Projects / Internships
6. Certifications
7. Additional Information

**Important:** There is no single mandatory structure for every candidate. The order should highlight the candidate's strongest and most relevant qualifications.

---

### 3. Understanding the Difference Between Sections

Do not confuse these three sections:

| Section | What it communicates |
|---|---|
| Professional Summary | Who the candidate is professionally |
| Key Skills | What the candidate can do |
| Work Experience | How the candidate has applied those skills |

**Example:**

- **Summary:** Administrative professional with 3 years of experience.
- **Skill:** MS Excel.
- **Experience:** Prepared and maintained Excel-based office records.

Each section serves a different purpose.

---

### 4. Practical Exercise

Consider our fictional candidate, Rahul Sharma:

- B.Com graduate
- Fresher
- MS Excel and MS Word
- Completed a college project involving sales data analysis
- Looking for an Office Assistant position

Answer the following:

1. Which section should appear immediately after his contact information?
2. Should he include a Work Experience section?
3. Where should he mention his college project?
4. Should he include MS Excel under Professional Summary, Key Skills or both?
5. Which section could be more prominent in his resume: Education or Work Experience?

---

### 5. Today's Assignment

Create a basic resume outline for Rahul Sharma.

Do not write the complete resume yet.

Just prepare the headings in the correct order and list the information that belongs under each heading.

---

### 6. Key Takeaways

- Every resume section has a specific purpose.
- Resume structure should depend on the candidate's background.
- Freshers should highlight education, projects and relevant skills.
- Experienced candidates should give appropriate prominence to work experience.
- Always prioritise information relevant to the target job.

---

**Aeraf CV Quality Principle:**

A well-structured resume helps recruiters find the most important information quickly. Good content deserves good organisation.
