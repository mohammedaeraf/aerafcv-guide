# Important Instructions for Resume Writers

When preparing resumes using these profiles, follow these rules:

1. **Accuracy:** Never invent qualifications, employment history, skills or achievements.
2. **Verification:** Ask candidates to clarify incomplete or questionable information.
3. **Honesty:** Never exaggerate job responsibilities or professional designations.
4. **Tailoring:** Adapt the resume to the target job description.
5. **Confidentiality:** Protect candidate information and use secure storage.
6. **Professionalism:** Use clear language, appropriate formatting and relevant information.
7. **Privacy:** Avoid unnecessary personal information such as religion, marital status or passport details unless legitimately required.

**Training Rule:** A resume writer's responsibility is to present a candidate's genuine qualifications and experience in the best possible professional manner – not to create a fictional professional identity.
